Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

The short version

XLTab processes every file locally on your device. It collects no personal data, transmits nothing, contains no analytics or telemetry, and makes no network connections.

What XLTab does

XLTab is a spreadsheet workspace. It reads and writes spreadsheet files (.xlsx, .xlsm, .csv) that you explicitly open or save. All processing happens entirely on your device.

What XLTab does not do

  • No collection of personal information or usage data
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or diagnostic upload
  • No accounts, sign-in, or registration
  • No network requests of any kind — the app works fully offline
  • No advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs
  • No access to your files beyond the ones you open

File access

The app opens only the files and folders you choose through the open dialog, drag-and-drop, or Windows file associations. Nothing is scanned, indexed, or uploaded in the background.

Data storage

XLTab stores no data outside the files you save yourself. There is no cloud sync — your spreadsheets remain exactly where you keep them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be published on this page before the corresponding app update ships.
